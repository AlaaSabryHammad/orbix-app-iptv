Questions

Good to know

Does Orbix include channels or content?

No. Orbix is a media player only. It doesn't provide, host or sell any channels, movies or series. You connect a service you are authorized to use, and Orbix plays it.

Which sources are supported?

Xtream Codes accounts, M3U / M3U8 playlist links, local playlist files, and XMLTV guides — from your provider or your own URL.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Orbix is free, with no sign-up, no ads and no tracking. Everything you add stays on your device.

How do I know the file is genuine?

The APK is signed by the Orbix developer. For either download, compare the SHA-256 fingerprint shown above with your downloaded file.

Which devices does it run on?

Android 9 or newer: phones, tablets and foldables, with layouts designed for each. And 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 PCs, with mouse and keyboard controls.
